    Know the threats before they find you

    Each lesson explains what the attack is, shows a real-life example, and ends with how to spot and stop it.

    Phishing

    Fake emails, SMS and calls that trick you into sharing passwords, OTPs or card details. Most of them give themselves away if you know where to look.

    • A deadline that pushes you to act now
    • A sender or link that only looks like the real one
    • A request to log in or "verify" through a link
    Read the lesson

    UPI and online fraud

    Collect requests disguised as refunds, fake customer care numbers and QR code scams. You never need to enter your PIN to receive money.

    Read the lesson

    Malware

    Software that steals data or spies on you, often hidden inside cracked apps and unknown downloads.

    Read the lesson

    Password attacks

    How guessing, leaked passwords and reused logins lead to stolen accounts, and how to stop them.

    Read the lesson

    Ransomware

    Malware that locks your files and demands payment. Backups are your best defence.

    Read the lesson

    Public Wi-Fi risks

    Open networks in cafés, stations and campuses can be watched or copied by attackers. Learn what is safe to do on them and what to save for later.

    Read the lesson

    Already been scammed? Act fast.

    Call the national cyber crime helpline 1930 as soon as you can. Then file a report online. Quick action gives you the best chance of stopping the money.
